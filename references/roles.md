# Bounded role briefs

Use each brief with the minimum evidence the role needs. Model names are preferred host bindings; resolve availability and disclose alternatives. Keep raw recordings private; provide the coordinator compact results and paths. Finish or interrupt completed child agents according to host lifecycle rules.

## Email researcher — Luna max

Input: selected professional's exact name, canonical profile, confirmed company/domain, permitted tools, private output location and authorized provider budget/ledger.

Find the work email through a catalog-documented provider. Record returned name/address, provider/call ID, identity and ownership basis, SMTP/catch-all status, verification date and retrieval timestamp. Do not construct guessed addresses. Reserve before dispatch and save the complete response before settlement. Complementary identity-positive/SMTP-unknown and SMTP-positive results need not conflict; negative identity or SMTP results remain material contradictions. Cached verification is not a fresh test. No sending.

Return one compact record per person: address or unavailable, evidence paths, current-role confidence, verification result, ownership limitations, exact recorded cost and unresolved charge ceilings.

## X/activity researcher — Luna max

Input: selected identity/profile, company, work-relevant purpose, professional source anchors, output location and authorized budget/ledger.

Find personal X identities using two corroborating professional sources before buying posts. Exclude employer accounts and same-name lookalikes. Retain authorship, canonical URL, publication/retrieval dates and source coverage. Distinguish person-authored activity from company reposts. Use LinkedIn/company evidence where X is unconfirmed, without asserting which platform is more active from uneven coverage. No public replies or DM-availability inference.

Return confirmed account or no corroborated account, bounded search coverage/exclusions, relevant dated hooks, uncertainty and costs. Do not fill missing evidence with fabricated handles.

## Dedicated writer — Sol medium

Input: one frozen evidence brief: purpose/role, sender facts and gaps, recipient relevance/authority limits, supported hook, application status if relevant, proposed small artifact, preferred channel/holds and voice constraints.

Write one role/purpose-first draft by default. Connect a concrete recipient responsibility to accurate sender evidence and one small proposed contribution. Use short paragraphs and one low-effort ask to share the artifact. Validate the problem rather than claim it exists. Keep controls, citations and channel limitations in the report, while preserving factual/proposal accuracy in the message. Length should fit the requested channel; do not treat a particular experiment's word limit as universal.

## Coordinator edit

Reconcile source truth and newer-versus-stale application facts. Keep adjacent experience distinct from formal system/HR ownership. Improve voice without introducing unsupported claims. Check an existing checklist/FAQ/process before prescribing software. In a concept artifact, define a sample path, owner, proposed baseline and stop/revise criterion; none is an achieved metric. Route material gaps to the correct researcher or writer.

## Independent critic — Astra low

Input: final candidate drafts/reports and necessary evidence. In an experiment, use neutral randomized writer labels and withhold the decoding key until judgments lock.

Assess factual accuracy, recipient relevance, useful contribution, readable voice, bounded ask and honest proposal/channel limits. Flag unsupported title/authority, stale evidence, inferred problems, invented experience and contradictory channel findings. Recommend specific edits and targeted research; distinguish blocking issues from preferences. State source/image inspection limits. A score is editorial judgment, not a response prediction.

## Evidence brief / handoff

The coordinator brief should include purpose/domain; source snapshot hash; sender facts and gaps; per-contact exact identity, source-supported role and authority limits; hook sources with dates/authorship; channel assessments; proposal and need-to-validate; voice and length constraints; cost/verification holds. Keep source evidence separate from interpretation. Later channel supplements apply equally to compared writers and do not silently change their frozen writing inputs.
